1. Who we are

Glogix is a brand of GUARDLOGIX TECH PRIVATE LIMITED, a private limited company registered in India (GSTIN 07AAJCG8899F1ZW), with its registered office in Pitampura, New Delhi 110034, India ("Glogix", "we", "us").

For the personal data processed in the Glogix Task Assistant, we act as a Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"). Where a business customer sets up a workspace for its own team, that customer decides what its workspace is used for, and we process the workspace's data on its behalf and under our agreement with it.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Glogix Task Assistant: the assistant you talk to on WhatsApp, Zoho Cliq, email and other connected channels, its daily digests and reminders, the shared task sheet, and the related websites and support channels. It applies to workspace members, people a member communicates with through the assistant (for example someone sent an email draft you approved), and visitors to our website.

4. How we use it

  • To run the service: create and track your items, send reminders, digests and notices, keep the shared sheet up to date, and answer your questions about your items.
  • To understand your messages, including voice notes, so the right item is created or changed (see section 5). Every new item is shown to you as a card and is saved only when you confirm it.
  • To deliver messages on the channel you chose, and to fall back to another channel you have verified when one is down.
  • To keep an accurate record of who did what (the activity log), for you, your workspace and for security audits.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, enforce sending limits, and fix problems.
  • To measure usage and cost per workspace for billing and plan limits.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We process this data to provide the service you or your workspace asked for (DPDP Act section 7, "legitimate uses", and your consent where it is required, for example to receive WhatsApp messages). We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or build profiles for anyone outside your workspace.

5. AI and voice notes

  • To understand what you asked, the text of your message (or the transcript of your voice note) and the minimum context needed, such as the names of your workspace members, are sent to an AI model provider. By default this is Anthropic (the Claude models). A workspace may choose another provider or a model that runs on our own servers.
  • We use these providers under their business API terms, which do not allow them to use our data to train their models. Text sent to them is treated as data, never as instructions to the assistant.
  • Voice notes are turned into text on our own servers; the audio is not sent to an outside speech service. The audio of a voice note used only to give an instruction is deleted after 7 days; the text it produced stays with the item. A voice note you attach to an item as a file is kept with that item.
  • The assistant never sends an email, invites someone outside your workspace, or makes a payment on its own. Anything that leaves your workspace needs a person to tap Confirm or Send.

7. WhatsApp

  • The assistant only messages you on WhatsApp after you verify your number and agree to receive messages. We record the date and the wording you agreed to.
  • Send stop at any time to stop all WhatsApp messages from the assistant. Send start to turn them back on. Your items stay in the workspace and you can keep using another channel.
  • The assistant is a task and follow-up tool for your workspace. It does not answer general questions and does not send marketing.
  • Meta processes WhatsApp messages under its own terms and privacy policy.

8. How long we keep it

  • Items, comments and their files: for as long as the workspace exists, so its history stays complete. Files of an item that was dropped are kept for 90 days, then the item's closer is asked before they are deleted.
  • Instruction voice notes: the audio is deleted after 7 days (section 5).
  • Calendar and mailbox tokens: until you disconnect them, revoke access at Google or Zoho, or leave your last workspace; then they are deleted.
  • Deleted workspaces: the workspace Owner can export and delete a workspace. After deletion it can still be restored from backup until that backup expires (1 year), then it is gone.
  • Inactive workspaces: after 180 days with no activity a workspace is archived (after warning its Owner) and restored on its next message.
  • Security and audit logs: 180 days.
  • Backups: encrypted, kept for 1 year, then overwritten.

When you ask us to erase your personal data, we remove it from the live service and it ages out of backups within the backup period above, unless the law requires us to keep something longer.

9. How we protect it

  • Each workspace has its own database with its own login, so one workspace cannot read another's data.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Calendar and mailbox tokens and backups are encrypted at rest; the keys are kept apart from the data.
  • Files are scanned for viruses before they are stored.
  • Every change to an item is written to a log that cannot be silently edited, and access by our staff is limited and logged.
  • Webhooks from WhatsApp, Zoho and email are checked for authenticity before they are processed.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell the affected workspace and you, and notify the Data Protection Board of India, as the DPDP Act requires.

10. Who can see your items

  • Items are shared with the members of the workspace by default.
  • An item marked Private is visible only to the people named on it. It never appears in anyone else's digest, search, counts or sheet.
  • The workspace Owner cannot read Private or Restricted items they are not named on. The Owner's audit export shows only how many such items exist per member, with no titles or contents.

11. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and other applicable law you can:

  • Access: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used, and who it has been shared with.
  • Correct and update inaccurate or incomplete data; most profile details you can change yourself by telling the assistant (for example "my timezone is London").
  • Erase data that is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example by sending stop on WhatsApp or disconnecting a calendar. This does not affect what was done before.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.
  • Raise a grievance with us (section 15), and if you are not satisfied, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

If you are a member of a business workspace, you can also contact your workspace Owner, who controls that workspace's data. We will respond to verified requests within 30 days.

12. Where data is processed

Our own servers are located in India and the United States. Some of our sub-processors (for example Meta, Anthropic and Google) process data in other countries, including the United States. We transfer data only to countries the Government of India has not restricted under the DPDP Act, and under contracts that protect it.

13. Children

The service is for adults using it for work or for managing a household. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date, and tell workspace Owners in advance of any material change.

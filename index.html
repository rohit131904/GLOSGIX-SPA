Platform

Built for analysts who don't have time to dig

Every module in Glogix exists to cut the distance between a raw log line and a decision your team can act on.

Real-time Correlation

Streams from every endpoint, firewall and cloud service are stitched together the moment they land, not batched hours later.

Attack Story Reconstruction

Instead of 400 disconnected alerts, Glogix shows one visual chain: entry point, lateral movement, dwell time, and blast radius.

Financial Risk Scoring

Every incident is translated into a projected monetary exposure, so leadership sees risk in numbers finance already trusts.

Auto-Ticketing

Confirmed incidents open, update, and close tickets in your existing service desk automatically — no manual triage relay.

Threat Intel Feeds

Every alert is auto-enriched against live IOC feeds, flagging known-bad IPs, hashes and domains before an analyst even opens it.

Executive Dashboards

A single Security Score and posture trend line CISOs can screenshot straight into a board deck.
